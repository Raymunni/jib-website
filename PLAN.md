# Jib website & blog — SEO plan

**Goal:** rank naturally on Google for Australian DIY decision questions, and turn those readers into Jib app installs.
**Success metric:** app installs attributed to the site (App Store campaign links + Google Play referrer links).
**Evidence behind this plan:** see `RESEARCH.md` (sources tagged evidence vs opinion).

---

## 0. The honest bits first

1. **This is a 6–12 month project, not a launch spike.** A brand-new domain with no backlinks typically sees near-zero search traffic for 2–3 months, first long-tail rankings around months 3–6, and meaningful traffic after 6–12 months of steady publishing. AI Overviews will also cut clicks even on pages that rank well.
2. **"AI photo → job plan" is not a moat.** At least four apps (FixMynd, YouFixedIt, HowToFix, AI Repair) already do it. The site must lead with what they don't have: **Australian licensing rules by state, Australian materials and quantities, and tracking the job through to before/after.**
3. **The best keyword gap is legal/safety content — which is high-stakes.** If a guide says a job is DIY-legal and it isn't, someone could get hurt or fined, and Google treats this as "your money or your life" territory. Every licensing page must cite the regulator, carry an "last checked" date and a clear disclaimer, and ideally be reviewed by a licensed electrician/plumber (a one-off paid review of the pillar page is worth it). The app's own "needs a licensed tradie" flags must agree with what the site says.
4. **SEO alone is slow.** Genuine participation where the questions are asked — r/AusRenovation, r/AusPropertyChat, Whirlpool forums, Bunnings Workshop — will likely bring the first readers and backlinks faster than Google. Answer the question properly; link only when the guide genuinely helps.
5. **Solo bandwidth is the real constraint.** The calendar below is ~18 pieces in 12 weeks. If that's too much, cut the number, not the quality — one thin page hurts the whole site.
6. **The apps aren't live yet.** Until they are, the CTA should capture a "notify me" email so early readers aren't wasted.

## 1. Positioning

**One line:** *Jib tells you what you can legally do yourself, what you'll need, and what it'll cost — then helps you plan and track the job.*

- **For:** Australian homeowners, DIYers and small-scale renovators at the moment they're deciding "can I do this myself?"
- **Against hipages/Airtasker:** they make money when you hire a tradie; Jib is on the DIYer's side — and honest when you *do* need one.
- **Against Bunnings:** Bunnings says "check your state's rules". Jib actually tells you, per state, per job.
- **Against AI clones:** Australian rules and materials, not generic US advice.

**Voice:** plain-English, practical, honest about limits. Written by a real person who does DIY, with real photos.

## 2. Keyword clusters and structure

Hub-and-spoke: each **pillar** page covers a topic broadly and links to every **cluster** page, and every cluster page links back to its pillar and to 2–3 siblings.

### Pillar A — "What can you legally DIY in Australia?" (priority 1)
Pillar: *What electrical, plumbing & gas work can you legally DIY in Australia? State-by-state (2026)*
Cluster pages ("can I legally DIY [job] in [state]"):
- power points & light switches · toilets · tapware · dishwashers · rangehoods · ceiling fans · waterproofing · gas appliances · hot water systems · smoke alarms
- State-specific roundups: plumbing in QLD, electrical in VIC, etc.
- Start with ~10 jobs × the most-searched states, **each hand-checked** — not auto-generated for every combination.

### Pillar B — "Do I need council approval?" (priority 2)
Pillar: *Do you need council approval? Decks, sheds, fences and retaining walls by state*
Clusters: deck NSW/QLD/VIC · shed size limits · fence heights · retaining wall heights · pergolas/patio roofs

### Pillar C — "What do I need for [job]?" materials lists (priority 2)
Pillar: *DIY materials lists: exactly what to buy for common jobs (Australian sizes)*
Clusters: raised garden bed with sleepers · timber deck · tiling a bathroom floor · paving (road base, sand, pavers per m²) · painting a room

### Tools (priority 3, build in month 2–3)
- **DIY legality checker** — pick your state and job → "DIY OK / licence required / certificate required", with the source. The web version of the app's tradie flag.
- **Bathroom floor calculator** — tiles + adhesive + grout + waterproofing in one (narrow combined tool; the head-term "tile calculator" is too competitive).

### Not now
- "[job] cost in [city]" pages — only once there's real aggregated cost data from app users. Doing it from a template is the classic scaled-content penalty.
- Head-term calculators ("paint calculator") — owned by Bunnings, Dulux and many microsites.
- US/UK content — month 4+ (UK Part P is a natural first expansion).

## 4. Site architecture

```
/                               App landing page (SoftwareApplication schema)
/app                            Store router: phone → correct store; desktop → QR code
/guides/                        All guides, grouped by hub
/guides/diy-legal/              Pillar A
/guides/diy-legal/<job>-<state>/  Cluster pages
/guides/council-approval/       Pillar B + clusters
/guides/materials/              Pillar C + clusters
/tools/diy-legality-checker/
/tools/bathroom-floor-calculator/
/about/                         Founder story, why Jib exists, how rules are sourced/checked
/contact/
/privacy/  /terms/              Move from raymunni.github.io/jib-legal (then update store listings)
/sitemap.xml  /robots.txt
```

- Short, descriptive URLs with the keyword; no dates in URLs (so posts can be refreshed).
- Every guide: breadcrumb, author box, "last checked" date, sources list, 2–3 related guides, in-text CTA + one end CTA.

## 5. Technical SEO checklist

- [ ] Static site, mobile-first, no heavy JavaScript; target Lighthouse ≥ 95 on mobile
- [ ] HTTPS + custom domain; one canonical host (www or bare) with the other redirecting
- [ ] `sitemap.xml` with only canonical, indexable pages and accurate `lastmod`
- [ ] `robots.txt` pointing to the sitemap; **not** blocking AI crawlers (GPTBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended)
- [ ] Unique `<title>` and meta description per page; `rel=canonical` everywhere
- [ ] Open Graph + Twitter Card tags with a per-page share image
- [ ] Schema: `Organization` + `WebSite` (site-wide), `SoftwareApplication` (home), `Article` + `Person` author + `BreadcrumbList` (guides). FAQ/HowTo markup optional — no visual payoff any more
- [ ] Images: compressed WebP/AVIF, explicit width/height, descriptive alt text, lazy-loaded below the fold
- [ ] Heading structure: one H1; each H2/H3 followed by a direct 1–2 sentence answer
- [ ] Internal links: pillar ↔ cluster ↔ siblings; no orphan pages
- [ ] Custom 404 page; no broken links (check in CI)
- [ ] `apple-itunes-app` Smart App Banner meta tag once the app is live
- [ ] Later (when useful): `/.well-known/apple-app-site-association` and `assetlinks.json` for deep links

## 6. Conversion funnel

```
Google / AI answer / forum link
        ↓
Guide answers the question (first paragraph)
        ↓
In-text CTA at the exact step Jib helps with   ← biggest lever
        ↓
Phone: Smart App Banner (iOS) / small custom banner (Android) → store
Desktop: CTA opens QR code → phone → store
        ↓
Store page (rating + first 3 screenshots do the work) → install
```

- **Before launch:** CTA = "Get notified when Jib launches" (email). One email when the app goes live.
- **Printable checklist** (e.g. "Bathroom reno: what's DIY-legal in your state") with a QR code on the PDF — one per pillar.
- **Trust:** real author, real photos of real jobs, sources on every legal claim, "no ads, no tracking" statement, privacy policy.
- **Keep the app's privacy labels clean** — tracking SDKs visibly reduce installs.

## 7. Measurement

| What | Tool | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Rankings, impressions, clicks, indexing | **Google Search Console** | Submit sitemap on day 1; check weekly |
| Bing + ChatGPT/Copilot visibility | **Bing Webmaster Tools** | Import from Search Console in one click |
| Site visits + store-link clicks | Privacy-friendly analytics (chosen in Phase 3) | Cookieless, so no cookie banner needed |
| iOS installs from the site | **App Store Connect campaign links** (`ct=` per content cluster) | Needs ≥5 installs per campaign to show |
| Android installs from the site | **Google Play referrer UTM links** | Shows under store listing acquisition |
| AI citations | Manual monthly check: search your top questions in Google (AI Overview), ChatGPT, Perplexity | Note whether Jib is cited |

**Monthly review:** which pages get impressions but few clicks (rewrite title/intro), which get clicks but few store taps (move the CTA), which rules changed (update + re-date).

## 8. Realistic timeline

| Period | What to expect |
|---|---|
| Month 0–1 | Site live, sitemap submitted, pages indexed. Traffic ≈ zero from Google; first readers come from forums. |
| Month 2–3 | First impressions in Search Console for long-tail legality/approval questions. A trickle of clicks. |
| Month 3–6 | Long-tail pages start ranking on page 1–2. Pillar A begins earning links from forums. First attributable installs. |
| Month 6–12 | Meaningful traffic if publishing stays consistent and pages stay accurate. Tools and pillars carry most of it. |

No install numbers are promised here — there's no reliable benchmark for a new app in this niche. Month 3 is the first honest checkpoint: if Search Console shows impressions rising, the plan is working; if not, revisit keyword choices before writing more.
