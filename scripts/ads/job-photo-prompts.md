# Job photos for the ad screenshots

Each is a photo "snapped in the app": the problem itself, no people, phone
realism. Saved as `public/images/jib/jobs/<name>.jpg` by
`node scripts/take-job-photo.mjs <name>` and pointed to from
`scripts/ads/job-photos.map` for the ad ids listed.

Prompt frame (swap in the subject):

> Generate an image, landscape 16:9. A realistic photo snapped on a phone by
> a homeowner in an Australian house: SUBJECT. Natural daylight, ordinary
> slightly imperfect framing, true to life colours. No people, no text, no
> logos, no watermark. Everything physically plausible.
