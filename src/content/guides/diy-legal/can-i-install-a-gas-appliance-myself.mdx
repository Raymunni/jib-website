Gas is the one area where the rules are the same everywhere and there are no exceptions for owners: if it involves connecting, moving or altering anything gas, it's a licensed gasfitter's job.

## The rules

| Job | Who can do it |
|---|---|
| Install or replace a gas cooktop, oven or heater | <span class="v no">Licensed gasfitter</span> |
| Move a gas appliance to a new spot | <span class="v no">Licensed gasfitter</span> |
| Install a gas bayonet point | <span class="v no">Licensed gasfitter</span> |
| Replace a gas hot water system | <span class="v no">Licensed plumber and gasfitter</span> |
| Plug a portable heater into an existing bayonet | <span class="v maybe">Allowed where the heater is legal in your state</span> |

- **Every state:** gasfitting, gas pipework, installing or moving gas appliances and installing a bayonet point all need a licensed gasfitter.
- **Victoria:** installing, moving or replacing a gas appliance needs a licensed gasfitter and always needs a compliance certificate.

## Why there are no exceptions

A gas leak or a poorly flued appliance can cause a fire, an explosion or carbon monoxide poisoning, and carbon monoxide has no smell. Appliances also need the right gas type (natural gas or LPG), pressure and ventilation. That's why gas work is licensed without exception.

## Getting a quote

- Photograph the appliance, its data plate, and the gas connection or bayonet
- Say whether you're on natural gas or LPG bottles
- For a move, photograph both the old and new spots, and measure the distance
- Ask for the compliance certificate to be included in the price

## What you can do yourself

- Use appliances as designed, and keep vents and flues clear
- Have gas heaters serviced regularly (every two years is a common recommendation)
- Turn off the gas at the meter if you smell gas, don't use switches or flames, get everyone out, and call your gas distributor's emergency line

Using a portable heater on an existing bayonet has its own rules, see [can I use a portable gas heater on a bayonet?](/guides/diy-legal/can-i-use-a-portable-gas-heater-on-a-bayonet/)
