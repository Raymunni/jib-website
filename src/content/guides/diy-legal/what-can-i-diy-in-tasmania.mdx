Tasmania goes further than most states on plumbing: CBOS lets owners do a list of low-risk jobs on their own property, including some like-for-like replacements other states reserve for plumbers.

We checked 23 of the most common home jobs against Tasmania’s regulators. Each row says whether you can do it yourself, and why.

## The quick list

**You can DIY:** change a light globe; replace a battery-only smoke alarm; plug an appliance into an existing power point; change a tap washer or jumper valve; replace a whole tap or mixer; replace a screw-on showerhead; clear a blocked sink or drain with a plunger; patch a hole in plasterboard; regrout tiles or replace shower silicone.

**Needs a licensed tradie:** replace or add a power point; replace a light switch; replace a light fitting or downlight; install a ceiling fan; install a hardwired smoke alarm; connect a hardwired oven, cooktop or rangehood; replace a toilet suite; connect a dishwasher to water and drain; replace a hot water system; install or move a gas appliance or bayonet.

**Check with the regulator first:** replace the plug on an appliance or extension lead; replace washers inside a toilet cistern; plug a portable gas heater into an existing bayonet; waterproof your own bathroom (membrane).

| Replace a screw-on showerhead ([guide](/guides/diy-legal/can-i-replace-a-tap-myself/)) | <span class="v ok">DIY OK</span> | Owners can replace showerheads and shower hoses like-for-like on their own property. |
| Replace washers inside a toilet cistern ([guide](/guides/diy-legal/can-i-replace-a-toilet-myself/)) | <span class="v maybe">Check first</span> | We couldn't confirm a homeowner exemption from your regulator, treat it as licensed work unless they confirm otherwise. |

## Gas

Gasfitting is licensed everywhere. Using an existing bayonet is what it’s designed for; installing one isn’t DIY.

| Job | In TAS | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Install or move a gas appliance or bayonet ([guide](/guides/diy-legal/)) | <span class="v no">Licensed tradie</span> | All gasfitting, pipework, installing or moving gas appliances, and installing a bayonet point, needs a licensed gasfitter in every state. |
| Plug a portable gas heater into an existing bayonet ([guide](/guides/diy-legal/)) | <span class="v maybe">Check first</span> | Plugging a portable appliance into an existing bayonet is what bayonets are designed for. Installing the bayonet is licensed gasfitting. Some states restrict unflued gas heaters, check the heater is legal to use where you live. |

## Building and finishing

Finishing work is generally yours to do, as long as it doesn’t touch the plumbing, wiring or waterproofing.

If water is getting behind the tiles, the waterproofing may have failed; that needs more than new grout. |

## Rules specific to Tasmania

These are the jobs where Tasmania’s regulator has said something specific:

- **Change a tap washer or jumper valve:** Repairing tapware on your own property is low-risk work owners can do.
- **Replace a whole tap or mixer:** Tasmania lets owners repair or replace tapware like-for-like on their own property as low-risk work (vegetable-spray mixers excluded).
- **Replace a screw-on showerhead:** Owners can replace showerheads and shower hoses like-for-like on their own property.
