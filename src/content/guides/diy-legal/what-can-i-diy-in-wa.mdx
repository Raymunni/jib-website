Western Australia changed its plumbing rules in March 2024 so owners can do a handful of simple repairs themselves. Electrical work hasn’t changed: WA’s advice is to never replace fittings and switches yourself.

We checked 23 of the most common home jobs against Western Australia’s regulators. Each row says whether you can do it yourself, and why.

## The quick list

**You can DIY:** change a light globe; replace a battery-only smoke alarm; plug an appliance into an existing power point; change a tap washer or jumper valve; replace a screw-on showerhead; replace washers inside a toilet cistern; clear a blocked sink or drain with a plunger; patch a hole in plasterboard; regrout tiles or replace shower silicone.

**Needs a licensed tradie:** replace or add a power point; replace a light switch; replace a light fitting or downlight; install a ceiling fan; install a hardwired smoke alarm; connect a hardwired oven, cooktop or rangehood; replace a whole tap or mixer; replace a toilet suite; connect a dishwasher to water and drain; replace a hot water system; install or move a gas appliance or bayonet.

**Check with the regulator first:** replace the plug on an appliance or extension lead; plug a portable gas heater into an existing bayonet; waterproof your own bathroom (membrane).

## Gas

Gasfitting is licensed everywhere. Using an existing bayonet is what it’s designed for; installing one isn’t DIY.

| Job | In WA | Why |
|---|---|---|

| Plug a portable gas heater into an existing bayonet ([guide](/guides/diy-legal/)) | <span class="v maybe">Check first</span> | Plugging a portable appliance into an existing bayonet is what bayonets are designed for. ## Rules specific to Western Australia

These are the jobs where Western Australia’s regulator has said something specific:

- **Replace or add a power point:** WA's advice is 'never replace fittings and switches yourself'. Use a licensed electrician.
- **Replace a light switch:** WA: 'never replace fittings and switches yourself'.
- **Change a tap washer or jumper valve:** Since March 2024, owners can maintain or repair a tap (not thermostatic mixing taps).
- **Replace a screw-on showerhead:** Owners can maintain, repair or replace a showerhead.
- **Replace washers inside a toilet cistern:** Owners can replace a cistern washer.
- **Clear a blocked sink or drain with a plunger:** WA owners can clear a blocked fixture or waste pipe with a plunger.
