A cracked or loose light switch looks like a five-minute job: two screws, a new plate, done. In Australia it's licensed electrical work, and it's one of the most common jobs people get wrong.

## The rules by state

| State | Replace a light switch |
|---|---|
| Queensland | <span class="v no">Licensed electrician</span> |
| Victoria | <span class="v no">Licensed electrician</span> |
| Western Australia | <span class="v no">Licensed electrician</span> |
| New South Wales | <span class="v no">Licensed electrician</span> |
| SA, TAS, ACT, NT | <span class="v no">Licensed electrician</span> |

What the regulators say:

- **Victoria:** Energy Safe Victoria names changing light switches as work that isn't DIY.
- **Queensland:** the Electrical Safety Office lists replacing a switch as licensed electrical work.
- **Western Australia:** the advice is simply to “never replace fittings and switches yourself”.
- **Everywhere else:** a switch connects to fixed wiring, and all fixed-wiring work is licensed across Australia.

## Why it's licensed

Behind the plate, a switch is connected to the home's fixed wiring, often with the active conductor looped through to other switches and lights. A loose terminal can arc and overheat inside the wall long before anyone notices, and a wrongly wired switch can leave a light fitting live even when it's “off”. That's why every state treats it the same as a power point.

## What you can do yourself

- **Change the globe**, including swapping to LED or smart globes.
- **Use plug-in smart devices:** smart plugs, plug-in lamps and remote-controlled plug adapters all go into an existing power point.
- **Clean or paint around the plate**, with the plate left on and dry.
- **Note what's wrong:** a switch that's warm, buzzes, sparks or smells hot should be left off and looked at by an electrician soon.

## Smart switches

Smart switches that replace your wall switch are wired in the same way, so fitting one is licensed work. If you want smart lighting without an electrician, smart globes and plug-in smart plugs are the DIY route.

## Getting it done for less

Electricians usually charge a call-out, so bundle jobs: a loose switch, a dead power point and a new outdoor light in one visit cost far less than three visits.
