When the hot water goes, it's urgent, and it's tempting to buy a unit and fit it yourself. In Australia that's not an option: every state treats it as licensed work.

## The rules

| Job | Who can do it |
|---|---|
| Replace an electric hot water system | <span class="v no">Licensed plumber</span> (and electrician for any wiring changes) |
| Replace a gas hot water system | <span class="v no">Licensed plumber and gasfitter</span> |
| Replace a solar or heat pump system | <span class="v no">Licensed plumber</span> (plus electrician for wiring) |

Queensland's list of plumbing owners can do themselves specifically excludes hot water systems, and NSW requires a licence for any plumbing work regardless of cost.

## Why it's licensed

A hot water system stores water under pressure at high temperature. It needs a working pressure and temperature relief valve, correct connections to the water supply, and in most homes a tempering valve so the water at your bathroom taps can't scald. Gas units add gas connections and flueing. A mistake can mean scalding, a burst tank or a gas leak.

## Getting it replaced fast, and priced right

1. **Photograph the model plate** (the sticker on the side of the unit). It shows the brand, model, capacity and whether it's gas or electric.
2. **Photograph the whole unit** and the pipes and connections around it, plus where it sits (outside, garage, cupboard).
3. **Note the symptoms:** no hot water at all, lukewarm water, leaking from the tank or valve, or water that runs out quickly.
4. **Send the same brief to two or three plumbers.** With photos, many can quote a like-for-like replacement without a site visit.
5. **Ask what's included:** removal of the old unit, a new tempering valve if needed, and compliance paperwork.

For a template, see [how to write a job brief tradies can quote from](/guides/features/tradie-quote-brief/).

## Signs it's time

- The tank is leaking, rusty at the base, or water is pooling underneath
- The water is discoloured or smells, or the unit is noisy
- It's more than about 10 years old and giving trouble

If water is leaking from the tank itself, turn off the cold water supply to the unit and, for electric units, the hot water circuit at the switchboard, then call a plumber.
