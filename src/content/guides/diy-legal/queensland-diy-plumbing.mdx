Queensland is unusually clear about DIY plumbing. The Queensland Building and Construction Commission (QBCC) publishes a list of "unregulated" plumbing work — jobs that don't need a licence. If a job isn't on that list, it's regulated work and needs a licensed plumber.

## The unregulated work list

According to the QBCC, you can do these yourself:

| Job | DIY? |
|---|---|
| Replace a shower head | <span class="v ok">DIY OK</span> |
| Replace a domestic water filter cartridge | <span class="v ok">DIY OK</span> |
| Replace a jumper valve or washer in a tap | <span class="v ok">DIY OK</span> |
| Repair or replace a drop-valve washer, float-valve washer or suction-cup rubber in a toilet cistern | <span class="v ok">DIY OK</span> |
| Replace caps on ground-level inspection openings of a sanitary drain | <span class="v ok">DIY OK</span> |
| Clean a ground-level trap grate | <span class="v ok">DIY OK</span> |
| Irrigation work downstream of a tap, isolating valve or backflow device | <span class="v ok">DIY OK</span> |
| Maintain greywater or on-site sewage irrigation systems | <span class="v ok">DIY OK</span> |
| Incidental unskilled work, such as digging trenches for a plumber | <span class="v ok">DIY OK</span> |

**Important:** unregulated doesn't mean anything goes. The QBCC says unregulated work must still meet the plumbing codes and standards.

## Common jobs that are *not* on the list

| Job | Who does it |
|---|---|
| Replace a whole tap or mixer | <span class="v no">Licensed plumber</span> |
| Replace a toilet suite or cistern | <span class="v no">Licensed plumber</span> |
| Replace or install a hot water system | <span class="v no">Licensed plumber</span> (and gasfitter for gas) |
| Connect a dishwasher | <span class="v no">Licensed plumber</span> |
| Install a new sink, basin or shower | <span class="v no">Licensed plumber</span> |
| Unblock a drain beyond the grate (e.g. with a machine) | <span class="v no">Licensed plumber</span> |
| Anything gas | <span class="v no">Licensed gasfitter</span> |

## Penalties

Doing regulated plumbing work without a licence is an offence under Queensland law. Business Queensland lists maximum penalties of 250 penalty units for a first offence, 300 for a second, and 350 penalty units or a year in prison for a third or later offence. Penalty units are indexed each year, so the dollar amount rises over time — at current values, a first offence can run well into five figures.

## The garden exception is genuinely useful

Irrigation downstream of a tap, isolating valve or backflow prevention device is unregulated. That means you can plan and install your own drip irrigation or garden watering system running off an outdoor tap — just not the connection work upstream of the tap itself.

## Doing it right

- Buy parts that carry the WaterMark certification for anything connected to the water supply.
- Turn the water off at the isolation valve or main before you start.
- If a "simple" fix turns into a leak you can't stop, turn off the mains and call a plumber — water damage inside walls costs far more than the call-out.
