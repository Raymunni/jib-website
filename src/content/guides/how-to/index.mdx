1. **Which method fits my situation?** The size of the hole, the type of mould, the age of the house.
2. **What do I buy?** An exact list, in Australian product sizes.
3. **Which tools?** Jib's [AI Toolbox](/guides/features/garage-tool-inventory/) remembers what you already own.
4. **How long will it really take?** Including drying and curing, not just the hands-on time.
