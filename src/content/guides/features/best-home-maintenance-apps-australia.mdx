Search "home maintenance app" and most of what comes up is software for property managers and tradies. If you just own a house and want to stay on top of it, the list is shorter than you'd think, and a few of the well-known names haven't been touched in years.

**Full disclosure: we make Jib.** So rather than tell you it's the best, here's what each app says it does, in its own App Store listing, plus the numbers anyone can check: who makes it, its rating in Australia and when it was last updated. All of it as of 15 November 2026.

## What each one is good at

**Jib.** You photograph or describe a job (the sagging gate, the black silicone, the gutter that overflows) and it writes the plan: the steps, what to buy with a picture of each item, the tools, the time and a rough cost. It flags any part of a job that legally needs a licensed tradie in your state and can write the brief to send for quotes. There are repeating reminders, a shared list for the household, before and after photos, and Visualise, which shows your own room with the job done before you start. It's free with 10 AI credits a month; Home Owner is A$4.99 a month. Best for: people who want help with the jobs themselves, not just a list of them.

**SureHome.** Made in Australia, and first released on the App Store in November 2026. You enter your address, answer a few questions, and it suggests the maintenance your home needs and reminds you before each task is due. It also walks you through a check-up of the house and helps you hire a local tradie for bigger jobs. Best for: a ready-made maintenance schedule.

**Homer.** A broad home management app: maintenance tasks with reminders, a home inventory, a timeline, expenses and documents like receipts and manuals in one place. Best for: keeping the paperwork side of a house organised.

**Under My Roof.** An App Store Editors' Choice for home inventory. It tracks maintenance, repairs and renovations alongside everything you own, with documents attached. Best for: insurance records and a detailed inventory.

**Patina.** A simple, calm list of the small seasonal jobs (filters, gutters, alarms, the water heater) that reminds you when each is due. Best for: people who want something minimal.

**HomeZada.** A long-running home inventory app from the US, built around insurance, contents and the items in your home that need maintaining. Its iPhone app was last updated in May 2022, so check it still does what you need before relying on it.

**hipages and Bunnings.** Neither is a maintenance app, but plenty of Australians use them for it. hipages is for finding and comparing tradies. The Bunnings app is for shopping, with project lists and in-store maps. Both work well next to a maintenance app rather than instead of one.

## How to choose

- **You keep forgetting the regular jobs:** any app with repeating reminders will do. SureHome builds the schedule for you; Jib, Homer and Patina let you set your own.
- **You don't know how to do the job:** Jib is the one built around the steps, materials and cost of each job.
- **You want records for insurance or selling:** Under My Roof, Homer or HomeZada.
- **You're going to hire someone anyway:** hipages for quotes. Jib and SureHome can both get you to a tradie too.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is there a free home maintenance app in Australia?**
All of the apps above are free to download. Most have paid plans for the full features. Jib's free plan includes 10 AI credits a month.

**Which home maintenance apps are made in Australia?**
Jib and SureHome are both made by Australian developers, and so are hipages and Bunnings. Homer, Under My Roof, Patina and HomeZada come from overseas developers.

**Do these apps work on Android?**
This comparison uses the Australian App Store for iPhone. Jib is also on Google Play; check Google Play for the others.
