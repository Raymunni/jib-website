"How much will it cost?" is the first question about any home job, and the hardest to answer from a search engine. Online cost guides give national averages that don't match your job, your house or your suburb. Here's what really drives the price, and how to get a useful number before you start.

## What drives the cost of a home repair

| Factor | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Materials** | Quantity and quality. A budget tap and a designer mixer do the same job at very different prices. |
| **Licensed labour** | Any step that needs an electrician, plumber or gasfitter adds their labour, and often a call-out fee on top. |
| **Prep and hidden problems** | Removing old tiles, fixing rot under a deck board, or finding water damage behind the grout. |
| **Access** | Second-storey gutters, tight crawl spaces and roof work take longer, and may need scaffolding or special gear. |
| **Where you live** | Labour and material prices vary between cities and regions. |
| **Finish** | A "good enough" patch versus a perfect, invisible repair. |

## DIY vs paying a tradie

The biggest single swing in most jobs is labour. That's why knowing which parts are legally DIY matters for cost, not just compliance:

- **Do the labour-heavy DIY-legal parts yourself**: strip-out, prep, patching, painting, tiling, clean-up.
- **Batch the licensed work into one visit.** Two or three electrical jobs in one call-out costs far less than three separate visits.
- **Buy the fittings yourself where the tradie allows it**, like the tap or the light fitting, and pay only for installation. Agree this upfront.

## Getting a real price for tradie work

A planning estimate isn't a quote. For anything you pay someone to do:

1. **Get two or three written quotes** for the same scope.
2. **Send photos first.** Many tradies can give a ballpark from photos, and Jib keeps them with the job.
3. **Ask what's included**: call-out fee, materials, rubbish removal, compliance certificate.
4. **Check licences** on your state regulator's public register before you book.
5. **Keep the paperwork** (the invoice and compliance certificate) with the job, for when you sell or claim insurance.

## Saving money without cutting corners

- Fix small problems early. Resealing a shower costs far less than repairing the rot behind it.

## Frequently asked questions

**How accurate are the estimates?** They're planning ranges, drafted by AI from your photo and description. Real prices depend on your supplier and tradie, so get quotes for paid work.

**Does Jib show prices for my city?** Estimates take your location and currency into account where possible, but local prices vary. Treat the range as a guide.

**Why is the range so wide on some jobs?** Usually because something the photo can't show could change the job, like water damage behind tiles, or whether a part can be repaired or needs replacing. The note explains what.
