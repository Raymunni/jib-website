Here's what each part of Jib does, and why it's there.

## Photo → full job plan

Point your camera at anything that needs doing, like a cracked tile or a sagging gate, and Jib works out the job, titles it, and files it under the right room. When you're ready, the AI writes the plan: steps, tools, materials, a realistic cost range for where you live, and how long it'll take. See what that looks like for [patching plasterboard](/guides/how-to/how-to-patch-a-hole-in-plasterboard/) or [clearing bathroom mould](/guides/how-to/how-to-get-rid-of-mould-in-the-bathroom/).

## DIY safe or tradie required

Every job gets a verdict in its top-right corner. **Green: DIY safe.** **Red: tradie required**, with the exact step that needs a licensed electrician, plumber or gasfitter. It's based on the rules where your house is, and it's strictly about the law, not difficulty. [How the verdict works →](/guides/features/diy-or-tradie-verdict/)

## AI Toolbox

Take one photo of your garage or shed and Jib lists every tool it can see. From then on, every job knows what you own. [How to inventory your garage in one photo →](/guides/features/garage-tool-inventory/)

## Tools and materials, per job

Each job has two lists:

- **Tools:** what you need, ticked off if it's in your Toolbox, with a Buy button if it isn't. Add tools yourself from your Toolbox with **Add a tool**.
- **Materials:** the consumables to buy, with quantities and where to get them.

## One shopping list

Materials from every job, plus the tools you've chosen to buy, land in one list grouped by store. Tick a tool off and it moves into your Toolbox.

## Reminders for the recurring stuff

Gutters, smoke alarms, air-con filters, deck oiling: set a job to repeat and Jib reminds you before it becomes a problem. Not sure what to set? Start with the [home maintenance checklist](/guides/home-maintenance/).

## Before and after, room by room

Every finished job becomes a before-and-after pair in that room's gallery, and a timeline of your home. It's handy when you sell (see [renovating before selling](/guides/how-to/renovations-before-selling/)), and satisfying the rest of the time.

## Built for your climate

Plans account for your local climate, soil, storm season and termite risk. The same job gets different advice in Darwin than in Hobart, because it should.
