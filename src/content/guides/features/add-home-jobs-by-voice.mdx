You notice most house jobs at the worst moment. You're hanging washing and see the gutter sagging. You're carrying a sleeping kid past the door that sticks. By the time your hands are free, it's gone.

Saying it out loud is the fastest way to catch it.

## How it works

1. **Tap +** at the bottom of Jib, then the **microphone**.
2. **Say the job** the way you'd tell your partner: "The back garden bed's full of weeds, and the side gate latch is broken."
3. **Stop talking.** Jib notices the pause and gets to work.

A second later you'll see "Added 2 jobs". Jib wrote a proper title for each, kept any detail you mentioned, and put them in the right space.

## What Jib does with what you say

- **Writes a proper title.** "The back garden's a mess with weeds" becomes **Remove weeds from back garden bed**.
- **Splits separate jobs.** Mention two or three things and you get two or three jobs.
- **Files it in the right space.** Say "laundry" or "front yard" and it goes there. If you don't have that space yet, Jib makes it.
- **Keeps the detail.** Colours, sizes and "before the party on Saturday" end up in the description.

From there it's a normal job. Open it when you've got time and let Jib plan the steps, materials and cost.

## Tips

- **Say where it is.** "The ensuite tap drips" files better than "a tap drips".
- **One breath is plenty.** You don't need to be precise. Jib tidies it up.
- **Somewhere noisy?** Hold the phone a little closer, or use the camera button instead and snap it.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does Jib record my voice?** No. Your phone's own speech recognition (Apple's on iPhone, Google's on Android) turns what you say into text, and Jib only ever receives the text.

**Does it need the internet?** Yes, to turn what you said into jobs.

**Is it free?** Voice add is part of Jib's paid plans, which come with a free trial. It uses one AI credit each time.
