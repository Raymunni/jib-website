You know the trip. There are ten jobs around the house, each needing a few things: a tap washer, a tube of silicone, some sandpaper, the right screws for the gate. You get to Bunnings with a toddler in the trolley, and the list is… somewhere. Half of it's in your head, some is in a text to yourself, and one item is on the back of a receipt in the car. You wander the aisles, buy three things, and remember the rest on the drive home.

That's the problem Jib's shopping list was built to fix.

## How the list writes itself

1. **Snap the job.** Take a photo of the dripping tap, the cracked tile, the sagging gate.
2. **The AI writes the materials.** Each job gets its own materials list, with quantities and where to buy them, based on what it can see and where you live. (See [how photo-to-plan works](/guides/features/photo-to-job-plan/).)
3. **Every job's list merges into one.** Materials from all your jobs land on a single shopping list automatically.
4. **Missing tools join in.** If a job needs a tool that isn't in your [AI Toolbox](/guides/features/garage-tool-inventory/), Jib asks before adding it to the list.
5. **Open it in the aisle.** Tick things off as they go in the trolley.

## Know what every item is for

The bit that makes it work in a real hardware store: **every item shows which job it's for.** "Silicone, for: re-seal the shower, Bathroom." "Galvanised hinges, for: fix the side gate, Backyard." No more standing in the aisle wondering why you wrote "M8 × 60" on your hand.

You can view the list two ways:

| View | Best for |
|---|---|
| **By store** | Walking the aisles: everything for Bunnings together, everything from the landscape supplier together, tools to buy in their own group |
| **By job** | Deciding what to prioritise: see exactly what each job needs, and buy for the ones you'll do this weekend |

## Buy once, never twice

- **Tick a tool off, it goes in your Toolbox.** Next time a job needs it, Jib knows you own it.
- **Bought items stay out of the way.** They drop to a collapsed "Bought" section instead of cluttering the list.
- **Add anything by hand.** Light globes, batteries, a new doormat: add general items too.
- **Edit quantities and items.** It's your list; change anything.

## Tips for a trip that gets everything

- **Snap before you shop.** A photo of the old part (the tap cartridge, the hinge, the light globe base) beats any description at the counter. Jib keeps it with the job.
- **Measure first.** For anything cut to size, add the measurements to the job before you go.
- **Check the Toolbox.** Borrowing a neighbour's drill is cheaper than buying one you'll use once.
- **Batch by store.** Use "by store" view so one trip covers everything from one place.
- **Plan quantities properly.** For bigger jobs, use our calculators, like the [bathroom floor calculator](/tools/bathroom-floor-calculator/), or the [materials guides](/guides/materials/).

## On your computer too

Sign in to [Jib on the web](/web/?demo) to review and edit the list from your desk, add items, or check what's needed for which job, then pick it up on your phone in the store.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does it work with Bunnings?** The list isn't tied to one retailer. The AI suggests where to buy each item based on what it is. Bunnings, a plumbing supplier, a landscape yard, and you can change any of them.

**Can I share the list?** The list lives in your Jib account, on your phone and the web app.

**Is the shopping list free?** Adding items yourself is free. AI-generated materials lists are part of Jib's paid plans, which come with a free trial.
