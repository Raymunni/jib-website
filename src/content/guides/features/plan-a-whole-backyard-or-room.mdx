Most backyards and rooms don't need one big job. They need eight small ones: re-oil the deck, replace a few boards, fix the fence, clear the weeds, plant a bed. Do them in the wrong order and you'll paint the fence, then knock it while you rebuild the deck.

Here's a simple way to plan the whole space, and how to get the same plan from one photo.

## Step 1: list everything the space needs

Stand where you'd normally look at the space and write down every job you can see, big or small. Don't sort them yet.

A typical tired backyard might look like this:

- Replace rotted deck boards
- Treat and seal the deck
- Repaint the paling fence
- Clear the weeds and replant the garden bed
- Lay a paver path to the clothesline
- Fix the sagging side gate

## Step 2: put them in an order that avoids doing work twice

A good rule of thumb for almost any space:

| Phase | Do first | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Safety and structure | Rotted boards, leaks, loose railings, anything that needs a licensed tradie | Everything else sits on top of it. Fix it once, before the pretty work |
| 2. Big surfaces | Decks, paving, painting walls and fences | Messy, dusty jobs that would ruin finished planting or furniture |
| 3. Finishing touches | Planting, mulch, furniture, lighting | Goes in last, so nothing gets trampled or splashed |

Two more ordering tips:

- **Dirty before clean.** Pressure clean, sand and strip before you paint, seal or plant.
- **Top to bottom.** Gutters and eaves before walls, walls before floors.

## Step 3: give every job a time and a cost

Add a rough time (an hour, a weekend) and cost to each job. Two things jump out straight away: the total, and the jobs you can knock off in a spare hour while the big ones wait for a free weekend.

## Step 4: split DIY from tradie

Mark the jobs that legally or sensibly need a professional. In Australia that usually means electrical work, most plumbing and gas, anything structural that needs approval, and some work near boundaries or pools. Book those early, because tradies often have the longest lead time.

## Common questions

**Does it work for inside rooms too?**
Yes. A kitchen refresh, a kids' bedroom or a whole bathroom works the same way: structure and wet areas first, surfaces next, finishing touches last.

**Can I change the order?**
Yes. The plan is a starting point. Move jobs around to suit your weekends and budget.
