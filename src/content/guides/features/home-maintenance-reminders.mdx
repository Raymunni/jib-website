Nobody forgets to fix a burst pipe. What gets forgotten is the quiet stuff that comes round every few months: the smoke alarm test, the gutters, the air-con filters, the termite inspection. Each one is small. Missing them is how you end up with an overflowing gutter in a storm, a smoke alarm with a dead battery, or termites nobody looked for.

The fix isn't a better memory. It's making the schedule remember for you.

## The jobs that come back (and how often)

From our [home maintenance checklist](/guides/home-maintenance/), with sources from fire services, regulators and manufacturers:

| Job | Repeat |
|---|---|
| Test smoke alarms | Monthly |
| Test safety switches (RCDs) | Every 3 months |
| Clean air-con filters | Every 3 months (more in peak season) |
| Clear gutters and downpipes | Every 6 months, more with overhanging trees |
| Vacuum smoke alarms | Every 6 months |
| Ease the hot water relief valve | Every 6 months |
| Termite inspection | Yearly |
| Clean and oil the deck | Yearly |
| Gas heater service | Every 2 years |
| Replace smoke alarms | Every 10 years |

Use the [maintenance planner](/guides/home-maintenance/) to build this list for your own home: tick pool, gas, tank, solar and so on, and it shows only the jobs that apply.

## Tips for a schedule you'll keep

- **Anchor reminders to habits.** Test smoke alarms on the first Saturday of the month. Do the gutters when daylight saving starts and ends.
- **Group the quick ones.** Smoke alarm test, safety switch test and relief valve check take ten minutes together. Make them one "safety check" job.
- **Snap a photo the first time.** Photograph the switchboard, the relief valve and the filter slot, and next time you'll know exactly where they are.
- **Put the pros on the list too.** Termite inspections and gas services won't happen unless someone books them. A reminder a month early gives you time.
- **Don't over-schedule.** A short list you keep beats a long one you ignore. Start with the five that matter most: smoke alarms, safety switches, gutters, termites and the gas heater.

## Why reminders beat a calendar

A calendar reminder says "gutters". A Jib job says "gutters", with the photos from last time, the tools you'll need (ticked off against your [Toolbox](/guides/features/garage-tool-inventory/)), the steps, and when you last did it. When the reminder goes off, everything you need is already there.
