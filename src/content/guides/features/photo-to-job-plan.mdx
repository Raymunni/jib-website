Jib started with a problem every parent knows. You walk through your own house and see ten things that need doing: a cupboard door hanging off, a dripping tap, a cracked tile, a gutter full of leaves. But there's a toddler on your hip and dinner on the stove, and you don't even have time to write them down, let alone work out how to fix them. Then Saturday afternoon comes around, you've got a spare hour, and you can't remember a single one.

So Jib works the other way round. **Snap a photo the moment you notice something. That's it; get on with your day.** Jib's AI works out what the job is, names it, files it under the right room, and writes the whole plan: steps, materials, tools, time and cost. When you do get a sliver of time, open the app, scroll through the jobs it's already planned, and pick one that fits the time you've got.

The hardest part of most home jobs isn't the work. It's the ten browser tabs before it: what's actually wrong, what to buy, whether you're allowed to do it, how long it'll take. Jib collapses all of that into one step: take a photo.

Everything is editable. If the plan says three coats and you know your wall needs two, change it. If you want more detail, ask a follow-up question in the job's chat.

## How to take a photo that gets a great plan

The better the photo, the better the plan. Five tips:

1. **Show the problem and its surroundings.** A close-up of a crack is good; a second photo showing where it is on the wall is better. You can add several photos to one job.
2. **Include something for scale.** A hand, a tape measure or a standard object like a power point helps estimate sizes and quantities.
3. **Use good light.** Open the blinds or turn the lights on. Shadows hide detail.
4. **Add one line of context.** "Want to replace, not repair" or "renting, needs to be temporary" changes the plan.
5. **Photograph the label.** For appliances, hot water systems and fittings, a photo of the rating plate or model number helps a lot.

## Worked example: a lifting deck board

Photograph a cracked, lifting deck board and Jib comes back with something like:

- **Job:** Replace a damaged deck board (Backyard)
- **Steps:** check the joists underneath for rot; remove the old board's screws; lift the board; cut a new board to length so both ends land on joists; pre-drill and screw with two screws per joist; oil the new board and the cut ends
- **Materials:** a length of matching decking, stainless or galvanised decking screws, deck oil
- **Tools:** drill/driver, drop or circular saw, tape measure, pry bar (with the ones in your Toolbox already ticked)
- **Time:** 1–2 hours plus oil drying time
- **Verdict:** DIY safe, no licensed work involved
- **Warning:** if the joist underneath is soft, the problem is bigger than the board

The decking, screws and oil appear on your shopping list automatically, grouped by store, alongside everything else you need to buy. See the full [timber deck materials guide](/guides/materials/timber-deck-materials-list/) for building from scratch.

## How accurate is it?

The plan is a strong starting point, not a guarantee. It's drafted by AI from your photo and description, so:

- **Check quantities** before you buy, especially for bigger jobs. Our [bathroom floor calculator](/tools/bathroom-floor-calculator/) and [materials guides](/guides/materials/) show the maths.
- **Costs are ranges**, because prices vary by supplier, region and whether a trade does the work.
- **Legal verdicts are general guidance.** Confirm with your regulator for anything borderline; Jib links to the rules it's based on in our [DIY legal guides](/guides/diy-legal/).
- **If something looks off, ask.** The job chat lets you question any step.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is photo-to-plan free?** You can photograph and track jobs for free. The AI plan, materials list, tools check, cost estimate and verdict are part of Jib's paid plans, which come with a free trial.

**What kinds of jobs does it work for?** Anything around the home: repairs, maintenance, garden and outdoor jobs, small renovations, and planning bigger projects. It will also tell you when a job, or part of one, needs a licensed tradie.

**Can I plan a job without a photo?** Yes. Type what you want to do and Jib plans it the same way. A photo just makes the plan more specific.

**Does it work outside Australia?** Yes. Costs use your currency, and the DIY-or-tradie verdict is based on the rules where your house is.
