Most renovation regret comes from the same place: you couldn't really picture it until it was done. The paint looked different on the chip. The deck felt bigger in your head. The tiles were fine in the showroom and wrong in your bathroom.

You can now take most of the guesswork out with one photo. This guide covers how AI previews work, the jobs they're most useful for, and what they can't tell you.

## What an AI preview actually is

An AI preview takes a photo of your space as it is today and redraws it with the work finished. A good one keeps everything that isn't part of the job: the same camera angle, the same walls, windows, fence and light. Only the thing you're changing changes.

It's not a 3D model or a builder's render. Think of it as a very quick, very realistic sketch of your own space, made in seconds instead of days.

## How to do it in Jib

1. **Snap the space.** Take a photo from where you'd normally stand, in good daylight. Straight-on shots work best.
2. **Add the job or plan the space.** One job (re-oil the deck) or several (deck, lawn and garden bed) both work.
3. **Tap Visualise.** Jib pictures the space with those jobs done, based on your photo.
4. **Drag the slider.** Your photo sits on one side and the AI preview on the other, so you can compare exactly what changes.
5. **Try again or tweak it.** Not quite right? Ask for a different colour, timber or layout and compare.

The preview is saved to your gallery with the space, so it's there when you're choosing materials or showing your partner.

## What it's best for

| Job | What the preview helps you decide |
|---|---|
| Painting a room or the outside of the house | Whether the colour works with your floor, light and furniture |
| A new deck or patio | How much of the yard it takes up, and timber or paver colour |
| Garden beds and landscaping | Layout, and how the yard looks once it's planted |
| New flooring or tiles | Tile size and colour against your walls and fittings |
| Kitchen or bathroom refresh | Painted cupboards, new handles, a new splashback or vanity |
| Getting ready to sell | Which cosmetic jobs make the biggest visual difference |

It's especially good for the "can we agree on this?" moment. A preview of your own backyard settles more arguments than ten Pinterest boards.

## What it can't tell you

An AI preview is an idea of how it could look, not a promise. Keep these in mind:

- **It doesn't measure.** Sizes in the preview are approximate. Use the job's plan and your tape measure for real dimensions.
- **It doesn't know the rules.** A deck over 1 m high, a shed near the boundary or a pool fence can need approval. Check the job's plan and your council before you buy anything.
- **It can't see behind walls.** Rot under the boards, damp in the plaster or old wiring won't show in a picture.
- **Colours on screens vary.** Always test a real sample pot or tile in your space before you commit.

## A simple way to use it on your next job

1. Photograph the space today. That's your "before", whatever you decide.
2. Preview two or three versions, for example two paint colours or a deck at two sizes.
3. Pick one, then open the job's plan for the steps, materials and cost.
4. When the job's done, take an "after" from the same spot. Jib keeps your before and after together on the timeline.
