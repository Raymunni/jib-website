Most quote requests look like this: "Hi, need an electrician for a power point, can you quote?" The tradie has to call back, ask five questions, and often come out to look before they can give you a number. Multiply that by three quotes and your week is gone.

A short, clear brief gets you better quotes, faster. Tradies prefer them too, because they can tell straight away whether the job suits them.

## What a good brief includes

1. **One line on the job.** "Replace a scorched double power point in the hallway."
2. **Where.** Your suburb and the room. Save your street address for the tradie you book.
3. **The scope.** What you want done, in two to five dot points.
4. **What you've noticed.** Burn marks, a smell, when it started, anything that changed.
5. **Photos.** A wide shot showing where it is, and a close-up of the problem.
6. **A few questions.** Access, timing, parts, and any certificate you'll need afterwards.

Keep it under a page. A tradie reading it on their phone between jobs should get the picture in 30 seconds.

## A copy-paste template

```
I'd like a quote to [what needs doing].
Location: [suburb, state], [room or area]

What needs doing
- [task one]
- [task two]

What I've noticed
- [the problem, when it started, anything relevant]

Questions
- Is a site visit needed, or can you quote from the photos?
- When could you fit it in?
- Will I get a compliance certificate when it's done?

Photos attached.
```

## Photos that save a site visit

- **One wide, one close.** The wide shot shows access and context. The close-up shows the problem.
- **Turn the lights on.** Or use your phone's torch for inside cupboards and ceiling spaces.
- **Show the label.** For hot water systems, switchboards and appliances, photograph the model plate.
- **Include something for scale.** A tape measure or your hand is fine.

## Questions worth asking every tradie

- Are you licensed for this work in my state? Can I have your licence number?
- Is the quote fixed, or an estimate? What could change it?
- Does it include parts, call-out and rubbish removal?
- Will I get a certificate of compliance? For licensed electrical, plumbing and gas work, most states require one.
- How long is the workmanship warranty?

You can check a tradie's licence on your state regulator's public register before you book.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the brief include my address or name?** No. Jib leaves those out on purpose. Add them yourself for the tradie you choose.

**Does it include a price?** No. The brief is about the job, not what you expect to pay. Jib's cost estimate stays on the job for your own reference.

**Is it free?** The brief is part of Jib's paid plans and uses one AI credit the first time. Reopening, editing and sharing it after that is free.
