Most of what saves a house in a bushfire happens months before, on an ordinary weekend with a ladder and a rake. The RFS talks about ember attack: embers land in leafy gutters, gaps under doors and wood stacked against the house. Here's the list, straight from the NSW Rural Fire Service.

## Why prepare, even if you'll leave

The RFS says a well prepared home is **more likely to survive** a bushfire, even if your plan is to leave early. It's easier for you or firefighters to defend, less likely to put your neighbours' homes at risk, and gives you more protection if a fire comes suddenly and you can't get out.

Check whether you live on **bush fire prone land** with your state's fire service, and use the RFS household assessment tool if you're in NSW.

## The house

From the RFS's Prepare your home list:

- **Clean your gutters** of leaves and twigs. Our [gutter cleaning guide](/guides/home-maintenance/how-to-clean-gutters/) covers doing it safely.
- **Install metal gutter guards.**
- **Repair damaged or missing roof tiles.**
- **Fit fine metal mesh screens** to windows and doors.
- **Fit seals around doors and windows** to close the gaps.
- **Enclose the area under the house.**
- **Repair or cover gaps in external walls.**
- **Consider a sprinkler system** fitted to the gutters.

Roof work and gutter guards are often best left to a roofer, especially on a two-storey house.

## The yard

- **Keep the lawn short** and the garden well maintained.
- **Cut back trees and shrubs** that overhang the house.
- **Clean up fallen leaves, twigs and debris** around the property.
- **Move firewood and anything that burns** away from the house. Our [firewood guide](/guides/home-maintenance/how-to-store-firewood/) has more.

## Water

- **Have hoses long enough to reach all the way around the house.**
- If you have a **pool, tank or dam**, put a **Static Water Supply (SWS) sign** at your property entrance so firefighters know there's water.

## Paperwork and the plan

- **Check your home and contents insurance** is up to date and the cover is enough. The RFS includes this in its list.
- **Make a bushfire survival plan.** The most important decision, energy.gov.au notes, is whether you'll **leave early** or **stay and defend**. Either way, the plan and the preparation go together. Your state fire service has a template.
- **Know the Fire Danger Ratings** and where to get warnings.

In a life-threatening emergency, call **000**.

## When to do it

Before your local bushfire danger period starts, and again through summer as leaves build up. Gutters in particular need doing more than once in a dry, windy season.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I prepare my house for bushfire season?**
Clean the gutters, fix the roof, seal gaps and fit mesh, enclose under the house, keep the yard short and tidy, cut back overhanging trees, have long hoses ready, and make a bushfire survival plan.

**Should I prepare my house if I plan to leave early?**
Yes. The RFS says a prepared home is more likely to survive, and protects you if a fire comes suddenly and you can't leave.

**What is a Static Water Supply sign?**
A sign at your property entrance that tells firefighters you have a pool, tank or dam they can draw water from.

**Do gutter guards help in a bushfire?**
The RFS recommends metal gutter guards as part of preparing your home, along with keeping the gutters clean.
