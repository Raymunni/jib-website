Electric blankets sold in Australia have to meet a strict safety standard. SA's Metropolitan Fire Service says the trouble usually comes from blankets that are old, worn, misused, or haven't been checked for damage. The check takes about 20 minutes, once a year, on the day the blanket comes out of the cupboard.

## The yearly check, step by step

Do this before the blanket goes back on the bed for winter.

1. **Lay it out flat** on the bed or a clean floor, and look over the whole thing.
2. **Look for scorch marks, kinks, creases or lumps.** MFS says these can mean a problem with the heating elements inside.
3. **Check the fabric.** ACCC Product Safety says to look for frayed fabric, worn covering over the wires, and exposed elements.
4. **Check the cord and controller.** Look closely where the cord joins the controller and where it joins the blanket. MFS says to check the switch for cracks, discolouration, heat marks or other damage.
5. **Do the hot spot test.** Spread the blanket flat, cover it with an ordinary blanket to hold the heat in, and switch it on high for **10 to 15 minutes**. Then run your hand over it. A hot spot means the elements may be damaged.

**Found damage?** Stop using it. ACCC Product Safety says to throw a damaged electric blanket away, because it can cause an electric shock or a fire. MFS suggests the manufacturer can check it if you're unsure.

## How old is too old?

MFS says to have an electric blanket **checked by the manufacturer once it's more than 5 years old**. If the blanket is old enough that you can't remember buying it, and it's showing any wear, replacing it is the simpler fix. SA's Country Fire Service lists keeping electric blankets maintained and replaced among its winter safety steps.

## Using it safely

From MFS and ACCC Product Safety:

- **Warm the bed, then turn it off.** Don't leave it on overnight, or on during the day. Blankets have started fires when left on unattended.
- **Use it under you,** unless it's made to be used as an over-blanket.
- **Tie it to the mattress** with its tapes and smooth out wrinkles.
- **Never tuck it under the mattress,** fold it over, or run it under the pillows. That can make it overheat and scorch.
- **Don't pile clothes or heavy things on the bed** while it's on.
- **No pins.** They can damage the wiring.
- **Keep it dry.** Never use it wet, and never with a water bed.
- **Watch the cord.** Don't strain or twist it, or let it hang where a bed leg or castor can crush it.

### Who shouldn't use one

- **Babies.** MFS says they can dehydrate and get burns at fairly low temperatures.
- **People who can't easily turn it off,** or who are sensitive to heat, including frail older people.
- **People with incontinence.**
- ACCC Product Safety says to get advice first if you have diabetes or are pregnant.

## Storing it at the end of winter

**Roll it, don't fold it.** Folding kinks the elements. Store it where nothing will sit on top of it, or leave it flat on the bed, switched off and unplugged.

Only wash it if the label says it's washable, and follow the instructions. Otherwise, MFS says to sponge it lightly when it's dirty and let it dry flat.

## Electric throw rugs

The same checks apply. MFS adds that throw rugs are made to go over you, not under you, so don't use one in bed or fall asleep under it. Don't cover the controller while it's on, and take the controller off before washing.

## Frequently asked questions

**How often should you check an electric blanket?**
Every year when it comes out of storage, before you use it.

**How do you test an electric blanket for hot spots?**
Spread it flat, cover it with an ordinary blanket, switch it on high for 10 to 15 minutes, and feel across it with your hand. Any hot spot means the elements may be damaged.

**How long do electric blankets last?**
There's no set lifespan, but SA's Metropolitan Fire Service says to have one checked by the manufacturer once it's more than 5 years old.

**Is it safe to sleep with an electric blanket on?**
No. Fire services and ACCC Product Safety say to warm the bed, then turn it off before you sleep.
