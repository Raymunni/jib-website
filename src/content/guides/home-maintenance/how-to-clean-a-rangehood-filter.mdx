Look up under your rangehood. If the filter has a sticky brown film on it, you're not alone: it's one of the most forgotten jobs in the house. It also matters more than it looks, because grease and oil built up on a filter are a fire hazard, right above your cooktop. The good news is it's a 15-minute job.

## What you'll need

- Hot water and dishwashing liquid
- A soft brush, such as an old dish brush
- A sink or tub big enough to fit the filter
- A clean towel

## How to clean a metal rangehood filter

1. **Turn the rangehood off** and make sure the cooktop is cool.
2. **Unclip the filter.** Most have a small latch or spring. Hold the weight with your other hand, because it can drop out suddenly and may drip grease.
3. **Soak it in hot soapy water** for about 10 minutes to soften and dissolve the grease. CHOICE suggests this is usually enough to shift most of it.
4. **Scrub gently** with a soft brush, working along the mesh.
5. **Rinse with hot water** and hold it up to the light. If you can't see through it, soak it again.
6. **Dry it completely.** Water can sit in the honeycomb layers of a mesh filter, so shake it out and leave it to air dry before refitting.
7. **Clip it back in** and check that it's firmly latched.

While it's out, wipe the inside of the hood and the underside of the canopy with warm soapy water. Don't poke around in the fan or the wiring.

**Don't use caustic cleaners.** Many filters contain aluminium, and CHOICE warns caustic products can react with it dangerously. Most oven cleaners are caustic, so leave them under the sink.

## How often should you clean it?

| How you cook | How often |
|---|---|
| Lots of frying or wok cooking | Every 2 to 4 weeks |
| Cook most nights | About monthly |
| Light cooking | Every 2 months |

Advice varies a little. CHOICE suggests every one to two months depending on how much greasy food you cook. Fisher & Paykel says at least monthly for its filters, and one Westinghouse manual says a maximum of four weeks to reduce the risk of fire. Your own manual is the final word, and if it isn't handy, monthly is the safe default.

## Can a rangehood filter go in the dishwasher?

Often yes, if your manual says it's safe. CHOICE says it can go in the bottom drawer, clear of the spray arm. Fisher & Paykel says to stand its filters upright. Dishwasher detergent can discolour aluminium over time, which doesn't affect how it works but won't look as good, so hand-washing keeps it looking newer.

## Metal or charcoal? Check which you've got

Not every filter can be washed:

| Filter type | How to tell | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Metal mesh or baffle | Silver, metal, catches the grease | Wash it |
| Charcoal (activated carbon) | Dark pad or cartridge, on recirculating rangehoods | Generally replace it, as most can't be washed |

Charcoal filters are used in rangehoods that recirculate the air back into the kitchen rather than ducting it outside. They remove smells, and once they're used up, they stop working. Check your manual for how often. Some rangehoods have a light that tells you when.

## Signs it needs doing now

- The filter feels sticky or greasy when you touch it.
- Cooking smells linger, or the kitchen stays steamy.
- The fan sounds louder but seems to pull less air.
- Grease is dripping from the filter onto the cooktop.
- The smoke alarm goes off more often when you're cooking. See [why smoke alarms keep beeping](/guides/how-to/why-does-my-smoke-alarm-keep-beeping/).

## If there's a grease fire

If flames start on the cooktop, **never use water.** If it's safe, turn off the heat, slide a lid over the pan, and leave it there. If the fire has spread to the rangehood or you can't control it, get out and call Triple Zero (000).

## What about installing or moving a rangehood?

Cleaning it is DIY. Installing one can involve fixed wiring and ducting, so see [Can I connect a dishwasher or rangehood myself?](/guides/diy-legal/can-i-connect-a-dishwasher-or-rangehood-myself/) before you start.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do you get thick grease off a rangehood filter?**
Soak it longer in the hottest water you can safely handle, with dishwashing liquid, and scrub gently. Repeat if needed. Avoid caustic cleaners on aluminium filters.

**How often should a rangehood filter be cleaned in Australia?**
About every month or two, and more often if you fry a lot. Follow your manual if it gives a different interval.

**Can I use bicarb soda on a rangehood filter?**
A paste of bicarb soda is often suggested, but it's alkaline, and it can dull or mark aluminium. Check your manual first. Hot soapy water and a longer soak is the safer start.

**Can you wash a charcoal rangehood filter?**
Generally no. Most are designed to be replaced, so check your manual or the part number.

**Can I run my rangehood without the filter?**
No. Grease then gets into the fan and duct, where you can't easily clean it, and it adds to the fire risk.
