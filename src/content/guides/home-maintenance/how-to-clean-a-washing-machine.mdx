A washing machine that smells is usually not dirty in the drum you can see. The smell comes from the places you can't: the folds of the door seal, the detergent drawer, the filter and a film of old detergent called scrud. CHOICE says it builds up when you only wash on cold, or use too much or too little detergent. All of it is DIY, and most of it takes a few minutes.

## What you'll need

- A soft cloth and warm water with a little detergent
- An old toothbrush
- A washing machine cleaner, if your manual calls for one
- A shallow dish or baking tray and an old towel, for the pump filter

## Step 1: Run a drum clean cycle

Run an empty hot cleaning cycle about once a month. This is the job that clears scrud and the musty smell from inside the drum.

1. **Empty the drum.** Check for socks, coins and hair ties.
2. **Choose the cycle.** Use Drum Clean, Tub Clean or the maintenance program if your machine has one. If not, use the hottest cycle. Electrolux suggests a short cotton program at 90 degrees for its front loaders.
3. **Add what your manual says.** Fisher & Paykel's front loader guide says to add washing machine cleaner straight into the drum. LG recommends a washing machine tub cleaner. CHOICE says a little detergent on the hottest cycle works and a special cleaner is largely unnecessary. Electrolux says to run its maintenance wash with no detergent, so check your model.
4. **Turn off auto dosing** for this cycle if your machine has it. Electrolux says to switch AutoDose off.
5. **Leave the door open afterwards** so the drum dries out.

**How often?** Most brands say about monthly. Bosch suggests about every three months. Fisher & Paykel's front loader shows a reminder after 100 cycles, and says to run it more often if you use eco or liquid detergents and low temperature washes. If yours smells musty, LG suggests running its Tub Clean once a week for three weeks in a row.

## Step 2: Clean the door seal (front loaders)

The rubber seal around the door is where mould grows first, because water sits in its folds. Give it a wipe after the last load of the day and a proper clean once a month.

1. **Pull back the fold of the seal** and look underneath.
2. **Pick out anything caught in it.** LG says to check the bottom of the seal, at the six o'clock position, where hair, lint and coins collect.
3. **Wipe off mould with hot water and detergent.** That's CHOICE's advice for existing mould on the seal.
4. **For stubborn mould**, LG suggests ¾ cup (177 mL) of liquid chlorine bleach in about 3.8 litres of warm water. Never use bleach neat on the seal, as LG warns it can make the rubber deteriorate. Never use bleach and vinegar together.
5. **Dry it with a cloth.** LG says to wipe the seal and door dry when you've finished washing for the day.

## Step 3: Clean the detergent drawer

Old detergent and softener goo up the drawer and the holes above it. Pull it out and rinse it every couple of months.

1. **Pull the drawer out** until it stops, then press the release tab. On Fisher & Paykel front loaders it's beside the manual dose compartment.
2. **Rinse it with warm water.** Electrolux says not to use detergents or bleach on the drawer.
3. **Scrub the recess** where the drawer sits, especially the holes at the top, with an old toothbrush.
4. **Let the drawer dry** before refitting it, then leave it slightly open between washes.

Electrolux says every two months. CHOICE says to scrub it at least yearly. If you've had a smell, do it now.

## Step 4: Clean the pump filter (front loaders)

Many front loaders have a pump filter behind a small flap at the bottom front. It catches coins, buttons and lint before they reach the drain pump. Clean it every couple of months, and straight away if the machine won't drain.

1. **Wait for the water to cool.** Electrolux says to let hot water cool first, and never remove the filter while the machine is running.
2. **Unplug the machine.** Fisher & Paykel and Bosch both say to disconnect the power first.
3. **Lay down a towel and put a shallow dish under the flap.** More water comes out than you'd expect.
4. **Open the flap and drain the water** through the small hose if there is one, then replace its plug.
5. **Unscrew the filter** anticlockwise and rinse it under the tap.
6. **Check the hole behind it** and clear out any lint or objects. Fisher & Paykel says to check the impeller behind the filter turns freely.
7. **Screw the filter back in firmly** so it doesn't leak, close the flap and plug the machine back in.

If the impeller won't turn or something is stuck that you can't reach, stop and call the manufacturer. Fisher & Paykel says only the hoses, detergent drawer and pump filter are designed to be removed by the owner.

## Step 5: Clean the lint filter (top loaders)

Many top loaders have a lint filter inside the drum, and CHOICE says to clean it after every wash. Not every top loader has one. Samsung says to check your manual.

1. **Find the filter.** Your manual shows where it sits on your model.
2. **Press or lift it out**, open it and rinse the lint off under running water with a brush.
3. **Let it dry**, then click it back into place. Samsung says the filter case should be dry before it goes back in.

Top loaders get the drum clean cycle in Step 1 too: LG's Tub Clean steps are the same for front and top loaders. LG's pump filter steps are for front loaders only, so check your manual before taking anything apart on a top loader.

## How often to do each job

| Job | How often |
|---|---|
| Wipe the door seal dry | After the last load of the day |
| Leave the door and detergent drawer ajar | After every wash |
| Clean the top loader lint filter | After every wash |
| Run the drum clean cycle | About monthly. Bosch says about every three months |
| Clean the seal properly | Monthly |
| Clean the detergent drawer | Every two months (Electrolux) to yearly (CHOICE) |
| Clean the front loader pump filter | Every two months (Electrolux), or when it won't drain |
| Check the hoses | Yearly |
| Descale | Every six months in hard water areas, if your manual says so (Electrolux) |

Your manual is the final word. Fisher & Paykel also says the inlet hoses each have a small filter, so wash out any dirt now and then when you have the hoses off. CHOICE says to check hoses for damage yearly and replace them if needed.

## What not to use

- **Undiluted bleach on the seal.** LG warns it can make the gasket deteriorate.
- **Concentrated bleach, pre-soakers and solvents.** Fisher & Paykel lists them among the products that can damage the washer.
- **Abrasive, acid, alkaline or solvent cleaners on the outside.** Fisher & Paykel doesn't recommend them.
- **Wire wool or scourers.** Electrolux warns they can damage the surface.
- **Vinegar, unless your manual allows it.** CHOICE says harsh products and vinegar can damage the rubber seals.
- **Bleach and vinegar together, ever.**
- **Too much detergent.** CHOICE says a third of the recommended dose can still give a good wash, and overdosing helps build scrud.

## How to stop it smelling again

These habits do more than any cleaner.

- **Take washing out as soon as it finishes.** CHOICE says clothes left sitting start to smell.
- **Leave the door and the drawer ajar** between washes. Keep children and pets away from an open machine. Fisher & Paykel warns they can get trapped inside.
- **Do a hot wash now and then.** Cold-only washing helps scrud build up, CHOICE says.
- **Use less detergent**, and the right type for your machine.

## When to call a professional

Call the manufacturer or a qualified appliance repairer if:

- The machine won't drain after you've cleaned the pump filter.
- The impeller behind the filter won't turn, or something is stuck inside.
- It leaks from underneath, not just from the filter flap.
- The smell is still there after several drum cleans. LG says a smell that lasts after multiple tub cleans needs a repair.
- It trips the [safety switch](/guides/how-to/why-does-my-safety-switch-keep-tripping/), or you smell burning. Turn it off at the wall.

Don't take apart anything beyond the parts your manual says are user removable.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why does my washing machine smell?**
Usually mould and scrud in the door seal, the detergent drawer and the drum, or washing left sitting. Clean the seal and drawer, run a hot drum clean and leave the door ajar between washes.

**Can I clean my washing machine with vinegar?**
Only if your manual allows it. CHOICE says vinegar and harsh products can damage the rubber seals, and some manufacturers advise against it. Never use it with bleach.

**How do I get black mould off the washing machine seal?**
Wipe it with hot water and detergent. For stubborn mould, LG suggests a diluted bleach solution, never neat bleach.

**Do I need a washing machine cleaner?**
Check your manual. Fisher & Paykel and LG design their drum clean cycles for a cleaner, but CHOICE says a little detergent on a hot empty cycle works and cleaners are largely unnecessary.

**Where is the filter on my washing machine?**
On many front loaders, behind a small flap at the bottom front. On top loaders that have one, it's a lint filter inside the drum. Some machines don't have a filter you can reach.

**Should I leave the washing machine door open?**
Yes, ajar between washes, along with the detergent drawer, so it dries out. Keep children and pets away from it.
